FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Wrexa Technologies offer?

Wrexa Technologies offers a comprehensive range of services including AR/VR development, metaverse solutions, custom game development with Unreal Engine, AI/ML solutions, blockchain & Web3 development, CGI/VFX production, HSE simulation training, and web & mobile app development.

What is pixel streaming, and how can it benefit my business?

Pixel streaming allows you to run high-quality 3D applications and experiences in the cloud and stream them to any device with a web browser. This eliminates the need for expensive hardware on the user's end while delivering stunning visuals and interactive experiences.

How can I collaborate with Wrexa Technologies?

You can collaborate with us through project-based engagements or by hiring our developers on-demand. Simply reach out through our contact form, and our team will schedule a consultation to understand your requirements and propose the best collaboration model.

How can I request a quote for a project?

You can request a quote by filling out our contact form with your project details, or by emailing us directly at contact@wrexa.com. Our team will review your requirements and provide a detailed proposal within 24-48 hours.

What is CloudSlash, and how can it help reduce my AWS server costs?

CloudSlash is our proprietary cloud optimization solution that analyzes your AWS infrastructure and automatically identifies cost-saving opportunities. It can reduce your server costs by up to 60% through intelligent resource management, auto-scaling, and reserved instance optimization.

What industries can benefit from Wrexa's HSE simulation VR games?

Our HSE simulation VR games are designed for industries including oil & gas, manufacturing, construction, railways, healthcare, warehousing, and fire safety. These immersive training experiences help reduce workplace accidents and improve safety compliance.

Can Wrexa develop custom VR and AR experiences?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating custom VR and AR experiences tailored to your specific needs. Whether it's product visualization, virtual training, interactive marketing, or immersive entertainment, our team can bring your vision to life.

What is Wrexaverse, and how does it work?

Wrexaverse is our metaverse platform that enables businesses to create immersive virtual spaces for meetings, events, training, and commerce. It features customizable environments, avatar systems, real-time collaboration tools, and seamless integration with existing business systems.

Where can I see examples of your previous work?
